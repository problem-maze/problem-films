# Problem Films — Production Rules v1

These are acceptance gates, not suggestions.

## Body mechanics

1. The production rig must include root, pelvis, spine, chest, clavicles, shoulders, elbows, wrists, thighs, knees, ankles, feet, neck, and head.
2. A planted foot must not slide beyond the shot tolerance.
3. Center of mass must shift before a step.
4. The receiving foot must show visible compression/settle when weight arrives.
5. Pulling force must travel through wrist → shoulder → chest/spine → pelvis → planted foot.
6. Shoulder and pelvis response should lag external force rather than move as a rigid block.
7. Removing tension must produce a small counter-motion.
8. Holds under tension must remain alive through breathing, micro-corrections, and continuing material tension.

## Ribbon physics

1. Ribbons are surfaces with visible width, not single lines.
2. Contact around wrist/ankle must be readable and collision-aware.
3. Surface twist must be visible where force changes orientation.
4. Tension should propagate from reel to body rather than change everywhere at once.
5. Unsupported sections need believable sag.
6. Force changes need rebound/secondary motion.
7. Ribbons must not pass through body or floor.
8. Release must end in gravity-driven fall and disorder, not a regular geometric net.

## Film 03 — 12 second gate

- Duration: exactly 12.000 seconds
- Frame rate: 30 fps constant
- Frames: exactly 360
- Native review resolution: at least 1920×1080
- No explanatory dialogue/text during the test
- Sequence remains the approved 0–12 second Machine beat; no new story events until this proof is reviewed

## QC truthfulness

Never claim native resolution, frame uniqueness, collision success, or full-frame inspection without an actual measured check and a stored report.
