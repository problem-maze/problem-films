# Problem Films — Style Bible v1

## Identity

Problem Films turns invisible human patterns into physical rules, forces, spaces, and machines. Meaning must come from action and consequence rather than exposition.

## Color system

- Deep background: `#040608`
- Lunar white: `#E1EBF8`
- Functional cyan: `#73CBE8`, used sparingly for system state, force transfer, or information that changes the action

The image should remain mostly dark. White is structural. Cyan is functional, never decorative.

## Character

- Faceless and gender-neutral
- Geometric silhouette
- Stable proportions across films
- Movement must show planted feet, center-of-mass transfer, pelvis/chest/shoulder lag, breath, and recovery after force release

## Camera

Prefer strong compositions and readable action over constant camera movement. Camera motion must reveal spatial cause-and-effect, not hide weak animation.

## Lighting

Use darkness to shape form. Favor moon-white rim light, restrained cyan bounce, and controlled specular response. Avoid all-over neon glow.

## Materials

Objects may look minimal but must behave materially. Ribbons need width, twist, sag, collision, tension waves, gravity, release, and secondary motion.

## Forbidden shortcuts

- Still images with zoom/pan
- AI morphing between poses
- Foot sliding
- Arbitrary line thickness changes
- Decorative effects with no story consequence
- Fast cuts used to conceal broken motion
- Upscaled low-resolution renders described as native 4K
