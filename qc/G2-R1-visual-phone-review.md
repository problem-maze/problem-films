# G2-R1 Visual Review — Real Phone Recording

Decision: **REWORK G2-R1 / DO NOT ENTER G3 YET**

Source reviewed: real-phone recording `Recording_20260910_054326.mp4`.

## What improved versus G2
- Head continuity is cleaner; the shark reads less like separate cheek/root pieces.
- Jaw-to-belly continuity is improved.
- Pectoral roots are less visibly detached.
- Caudal root continuity is better.
- The 12-second staging exposes the anatomy clearly enough for honest review.

## Remaining blockers

### 1. Head / snout proportions
- The frontal head is still too round and bulbous.
- The snout lacks the flatter, broader great-white wedge and stronger lateral cheek taper.
- Forehead-to-rostrum transition remains too smooth.

### 2. Mouth / jaw silhouette
- The mouth still reads as a graphic smiling slot during approach.
- The lower-jaw opening has too much visible black cavity relative to facial planes.
- Jaw corners need more natural hinge volume and less symmetric arc.

### 3. Eye anatomy
- Eyes remain visually button-like and too isolated from brow/cheek anatomy.
- Socket placement still lacks the protective lateral/head-plane relationship expected in a great-white silhouette.
- G3 can fix optics, but G2 must first fix socket placement/scale.

### 4. Gills
- The five slits are still weak or nearly invisible in the close pass.
- They need stronger recessed geometry and better spacing/arc on the shoulder.

### 5. Pectoral fins
- They remain too straight and bar-like from frontal/three-quarter views.
- Root thickness improved, but span/sweep/taper still read mechanically.

### 6. Dorsal fin
- The dorsal remains too tall/thin and blade-like relative to the body mass.
- Root transition needs a broader base and slightly lower peak.

### 7. Belly / countershading edge
- The white ventral patch still forms a hard irregular polygonal edge under the jaw and along the flank.
- Some of this is shader/material mapping, but the close-pass read is strong enough that continuity cannot be judged cleanly until the anatomy-dependent boundary is stabilized.
- Do not solve this with texture noise alone.

### 8. Caudal silhouette
- Tail continuity is improved, but the lobes still read like large flat vertical blades in departure.
- Peduncle-to-lobe thickness and leading/trailing curvature need refinement.

## Scope boundary
Do not move to G3 yet. The remaining problems are primarily silhouette, proportion and anatomical continuity, not micro-material detail.

## Correct next operation
**G2-R2 — Great White Proportion Lock**

Focus only on:
- flatter/broader rostrum and cheek wedge;
- jaw-corner/mandible integration;
- eye socket placement;
- deeper/repositioned gill recesses;
- pectoral sweep/taper;
- lower/wider dorsal;
- caudal lobe thickness/curvature;
- stabilize ventral anatomical boundary so G3 materials have a clean surface to work on.

G0B runtime, G1 shot structure and G2 performance evidence remain locked.
