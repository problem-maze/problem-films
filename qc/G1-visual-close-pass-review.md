# G1 Visual Review — Close Pass

Decision: **REWORK VISUAL / G2 ANATOMY SCOPE APPROVED**

Source reviewed:
- user phone screen recording of the G1 12-second proof;
- close-pass region inspected around the measured 6–9 second proof segment.

## What works
- Shot staging is readable: emergence → approach → close pass → departure.
- The shark remains legible against the dark ocean and the environment preserves depth cues.
- The side pass gives enough exposure to diagnose the model honestly.
- Performance evidence already passed separately on the target phone.

## Why G1 does not visually pass
The shark still reads as a procedural CG prototype rather than a physically convincing great white at close range.

Observed defects:
1. **Head / snout**
   - front mass is too rounded/balloon-like;
   - snout transition into cranial volume is too smooth and generic;
   - cheek/jaw volume does not create a convincing great-white wedge.

2. **Mouth / teeth**
   - mouth reads as a dark outlined opening rather than a volumetric jaw cavity;
   - tooth spacing/size is too uniform and graphic;
   - lip/gum/jaw integration is missing, making the mouth look attached to the surface.

3. **Eye**
   - eye reads as a small black dot;
   - little visible socket/cornea depth or wet reflective structure.

4. **Gills**
   - gill slits read as straight dark lines drawn on the side;
   - insufficient groove depth, curvature, spacing variation and integration into the shoulder volume.

5. **Pectoral root / fins**
   - pectoral transition into the body is too thin and blade-like;
   - root lacks shoulder mass and muscular blending.

6. **Torso / countershading**
   - body surface is too smooth and simplified at close range;
   - light/dark countershading boundary is too clean and synthetic;
   - local anatomical planes are not strong enough.

7. **Peduncle / caudal tail**
   - caudal peduncle becomes too narrow and mechanically pinched;
   - tail silhouette reads overly symmetric/graphic;
   - upper/lower lobe character is not convincing enough for a great white.

8. **Motion read**
   - the close pass still feels like a rigid model translated through the scene more than a heavy animal generating force through the body;
   - this will be handled after the anatomy foundation in the dedicated locomotion gate.

## Gate result
- G1 phone performance: PASS.
- G1 close-pass realism: REWORK.
- Do not fake a G1 PASS.

The proof has completed its diagnostic purpose. Rather than cosmetically patching the same prototype, the correct next operation is **G2 Shark Anatomy Rebuild** using the defects above as the explicit scope.

G0B runtime remains locked and must not regress.
